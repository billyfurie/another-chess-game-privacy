# Privacy Policy - Another Chess Game

**Last updated: October 25, 2026**

Another Chess Game respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you play the game and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

Another Chess Game uses GameAnalytics, a third-party analytics service, to understand gameplay and improve the game.

The information collected includes:

- **A random player ID.** It’s created when you first open the game and isn’t tied to your name, email or any account.
- **Gameplay data:** the King and difficulty you pick, regions started, completed or failed, turns played, captures, pieces lost, gold earned, how far you got and how each run ended, other gameplay events and statistics.
- **Session data:** when you play and for how long.
- **Device information:** device model, operating system and version, game version, connection type, and an approximate country based on your IP address. On iOS this may include Apple’s identifier for vendor (IDFV).

We do not require players to create an account or provide their name or email address to play.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Analytics information is used to:

- Understand how players interact with the game
- Identify bugs and gameplay issues
- Improve game balance and performance
- Evaluate features and overall player experience

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use **GameAnalytics** to process gameplay analytics information on our behalf.

For more information about its data practices, visit:

https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust

## 4. Game Saves

Game progress, unlocks, settings, and records are stored locally on your device.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game data can be removed by deleting the app.

Analytics data is processed and retained by GameAnalytics according to its applicable data retention practices.

You may contact us to request information about, or deletion of, analytics data associated with your use of the game, subject to applicable identification and technical limitations.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our game or data practices. Updates will be posted on this page.
